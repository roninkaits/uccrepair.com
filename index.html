Time is critical with liquid damage. What you do in the first few minutes can mean the difference between a recoverable machine and a dead one. Here's what to do:

✓ DO:

• Power it off immediately — hold the power button until it shuts down completely.

• Use paper towels to dry the outside of the device as much as possible. Tilt it to let liquid drain out.

• Bring it to a repair shop ASAP. The sooner a technician can open it and dry the internals, the better the odds of saving it. Call us at 858-638-7392.

• If repair shops are closed, place a fan blowing directly on the device to help air dry it until you can get it in.

✗ DO NOT:

• Do NOT try to turn it on — powering on a wet device can short-circuit the motherboard and cause permanent damage.

• Do NOT plug it into a charger — sending electricity through wet components makes everything worse.

• Do NOT put it in rice. This is a myth. Rice doesn't fix liquid damage — it just wastes critical time and can leave starch and dust inside your machine. Get it to a professional instead.
